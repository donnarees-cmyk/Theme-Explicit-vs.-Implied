The Willow and the Oak

High upon a windy Florida ridge stood a tall, mighty Oak tree. Beside him grew a slender Willow tree. The Oak boasted daily to the forest animals, "Look at my thick trunk and sturdy roots! I stand firm against everything and bend for no one."

The gentle Willow whispered, "I may be soft, but I yield gracefully when the strong winds sweep from the gulf coast." The Oak scoffed at the Willow's humble attitude.

One evening, a powerful storm raged across the ridge. Fierce winds battered the trees. The Oak stood rigid, fighting every gust with all his strength, refusing to bow. With a violent crash, the proud Oak snapped at his base.

Meanwhile, the flexible Willow bent low with the wind, letting the storm pass over her. When morning sun illuminated the forest, the Willow straightened gently, unharmed. The fallen Oak sighed, realizing that true strength lies not in stubborn rigidity, but in adaptability.
